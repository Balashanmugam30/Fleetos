# Fleetos ATLAS Multilingual Indic Voice Operations

Product: **Fleetos** (Agentic Multimodal Fleet Intelligence Platform)  
Voice Agent: **ATLAS**

---

## Multilingual & Code-Mixing Capabilities

ATLAS leverages **Sarvam Voice Agents** to provide real-time conversational telephony in Indian regional languages with natural code-mixing (Tanglish / Hinglish).

### Supported Indic Languages
1. **Tamil (`ta-IN`)**: *"Anna, loading la 45 minutes delay aagudhu."*
2. **Hindi (`hi-IN`)**: *"Bhaiya, loading station pe 45 minute ka delay hai."*
3. **English (`en-IN`)**: *"Hello, there is a 45-minute loading delay at the hub."*
4. **Telugu (`te-IN`)**
5. **Kannada (`kn-IN`)**
6. **Malayalam (`ml-IN`)**
7. **Bengali (`bn-IN`)**
8. **Marathi (`mr-IN`)**
9. **Gujarati (`gu-IN`)**
10. **Punjabi (`pa-IN`)**
11. **Odia (`or-IN`)**

### Automatic Language Detection (`AUTO`)
When the language selector is set to `AUTO`, Sarvam Voice Agents dynamically detects the driver's spoken language and responds in the matching dialect/code-mixed language.
