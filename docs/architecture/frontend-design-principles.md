# Fleetos Frontend Design Principles & Visual Language

Product: **Fleetos**  
Visual Aesthetic: **Bright, Modern Enterprise Logistics Software**

---

## Mandated Visual Guidelines

1. **Light Theme First**: Clean white/light gray background surfaces (`#f8fafc`, `#ffffff`), dark neutral text (`#0f172a`), subtle slate borders (`#e2e8f0`).
2. **Prohibited Aesthetics**: DO NOT use dark hacker/cyberpunk themes, neon purple/cyan gradients, glowing AI circles, or heavy glassmorphism.
3. **Restrained Color Accents**:
   - Success / Optimal: Emerald Green (`#059669`)
   - Warning / Risk: Amber Yellow (`#d97706`)
   - Critical / Breach: Crimson Red (`#dc2626`)
   - Operational Actions: Brand Blue/Teal (`#0284c7`)
4. **Legibility & Layout**: Generous padding, high contrast typography, card-based enterprise control tower structure.
